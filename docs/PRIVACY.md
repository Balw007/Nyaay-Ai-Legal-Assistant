# Privacy and confidentiality (summary for firms)

**Where data lives.** Your firm's database (matters, orders, drafts, notes) is on your own PC or on the server
you host. Run the hosted version in an Indian region to keep client data in India.

**Who can see it.** Only signed-in members of your firm. Every row is scoped to the firm of the signed-in user.
Roles: owner, admin, advocate, clerk. Every approval, confirmation, sign-off and export is in the audit log.

**AI.** AI is optional and can be switched off per firm (Settings → Firm & privacy). When on:
- calls go to Anthropic's API; under its commercial terms, API data is not used for model training;
- Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, emails, IFSC and account numbers are replaced with tokens before sending and
  restored in the reply; party names are sent because the AI cannot read an order without them;
- the external Indian Kanoon check sends only the citation and case name, never the draft.

**Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023).** Admins can export everything the firm has stored (Settings → Data) and
permanently erase a matter (Edit matter → Erase). Erasure removes orders, tasks, deadlines, notes, drafts,
saved authorities and citation checks for that matter.

**Get client consent** before AI processing of a client's documents, and keep AI off for matters where it
has not been given.
